OUR MANIFESTO

The cockroach creed: principles for a new India built by the people, for the people.

Education Reform

Free, quality education for all. End the coaching mafia. Practical skills over rote learning.

We demand curriculum reform that focuses on critical thinking, digital literacy, and mental well-being. No student should be burdened by debt for pursuing knowledge.

Anti Corruption

Transparent governance. Real accountability. Zero tolerance for bribery at any level.

Blockchain-based public records, citizen oversight committees, and whistleblower protection laws to ensure every rupee serves the people.

Youth Employment

Guaranteed internships, startup incubation, and skills training for every young Indian.

Partnering with industries to create 10 million new jobs in green tech, digital services, and creative industries over the next 5 years.

Transparent Governance

Open data, participatory budgeting, and real-time tracking of public projects.

Every government expenditure visible to citizens. Decisions made with community input. Power returned to the people where it belongs.

Mental Health Awareness

End the stigma. Free counseling in every college. Mental health as a fundamental right.

Training 100,000 youth mental health first-aid responders. Integrating emotional wellness into education and workplace policies.

Digital Freedom

Net neutrality, data privacy, and digital rights protected in the constitution.

Internet access as a fundamental right. Protection from surveillance capitalism. Digital literacy programs for all ages.

Exam System Accountability

Fair evaluation, transparent processes, and alternatives to high-stakes testing.

Reforming examination systems to reduce pressure, prevent leaks, and value diverse talents beyond memorization.

Student Rights

Protection from harassment, fair disciplinary processes, and student representation in governance.

Establishing independent student ombudsmen at every institution. Ensuring safe, inclusive campuses where every voice matters.
